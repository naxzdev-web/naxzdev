What it does

Every rule is on the post, so nobody has to ask.

Set the requirements once when you start the giveaway. Crimson Bot lists them in the message and enforces them when someone clicks Enter.

Role rules

Require a role to enter, block another role, and let chosen roles bypass the other requirements. Bypass roles never skip the blocklist.

Bonus entries

Give boosters, supporters or any role extra entries per person, from 2x up to 100x.

Message goals

Ask for a number of messages sent since the giveaway began, today, this week or all time. Cooldowns, daily limits and duplicate blocking keep it fair.

Claim window

Winners get a Claim button and a deadline you choose. Miss it and the bot draws someone else, automatically.

Open entry list

The Participants button shows everyone who entered, their entry counts, the totals, and the viewer's own chance of winning.

Ticket handoff

After a prize is claimed, the bot points the winner to your ticket channel with a link button, so your support team can deliver it.

How a giveaway runs

Four steps, and only the first is yours.

Start it

Run /giveaway with a prize and a duration like 2h or 1d12h. Add rules if you want them.

Members enter

People click Enter. The bot checks roles and message goals, then counts their entries live.

Winners drawn

When time is up, winners are picked at random, weighted by entries, from members who still qualify.

Prize claimed

Each winner clicks Claim before the deadline, or the bot rerolls. Claimed winners get your ticket link.

Commands

A short list you can learn in a minute.

Admin commands need the Manage Server permission. Slash commands show their options as you type them.

Giveaways

/giveawayStart a giveaway with any mix of rules.
/giveaway_endEnd one now and draw the winners.
/giveaway_cancelCancel one without drawing.
/giveaway_rerollDraw one more winner for a finished giveaway.
/giveaway_listSee the active giveaways in your server.

Message counter

/message counter enableStart counting messages in your server.
/message counter disableStop counting. Existing counts are kept.
/message counter spam-filtersCooldown, daily limit, minimum length, duplicates, ignored channels.
/message counter statsToday, this week and all-time counts for a member.
/message counter leaderboardTop members for today, this week or all time.
/message counter resetReset one member or the whole server.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What permissions does Crimson Bot need?
View Channel, Send Messages and Embed Links in the channels where you post giveaways. Members who run admin commands need the Manage Server permission.
Do giveaways survive a restart?
Yes. Giveaways and their entries are saved to disk. After a restart the bot picks up open giveaways where they left off, and the buttons keep working.
Can people farm messages to qualify?
The spam filters are there for that. You can set a cooldown between counted messages, a daily limit per member, a minimum message length, duplicate blocking, and channels to ignore. Minimum length and duplicate blocking need the Message Content intent turned on for the bot.
What happens if a winner never claims?
When the claim time runs out, the bot marks that winner as unclaimed and draws another eligible member. This repeats until the prize is claimed or nobody is left to draw.
Can I stop certain members from entering?
Set a blocked role when you start the giveaway. Members with that role can't enter, and they're skipped at draw time if they got the role after entering.
